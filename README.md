## How tools get listed

- **Live product only.** No waitlists, no "coming soon."
- **Public pricing or a real free tier.** If a vendor is enterprise-sales-only, we still list it, tagged `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`, so you know what you're getting into before you book a demo.
- **Actually used for finance or accounting**, not a general-purpose tool with one relevant use case bolted on.
- **Reviewed quarterly.** Dead links and abandoned products are removed — see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **No pay-to-list.** Sponsored placements are labeled `Sponsored` and never displace a better-fit free entry — see [Sponsors](#sponsors).

*Last full review: August 2026.*

## AI Tools for Bookkeeping & Accounting Automation

*AI bookkeeping tools automatically categorize transactions, reconcile bank accounts, and draft financial statements so accountants spend less time on data entry.*

- **[Digits](https://digits.com/pricing)** — AI-native general ledger that auto-categorizes transactions and drafts financials for small businesses and accounting firms. `Paid`
- **[Docyt](https://www.docyt.com/pricing-plans/)** — AI accounting platform that captures invoices, reconciles revenue, and automates bill pay for multi-location businesses and accounting firms. `Paid`
- **[Puzzle](https://puzzle.io/pricing)** — AI-powered accounting platform for startups that automates transaction categorization, reconciliations, and month-end close. `Paid`
- **[QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/)** — Intuit's small-business accounting software with AI agents that automate bookkeeping, bill pay, invoicing, and payroll. `Paid`
- **[Truewind](https://www.truewind.ai/product/ai-bookkeeping)** — AI bookkeeping co-pilot for accounting firms that auto-codes transactions, reconciles accounts, and drafts close workpapers. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Xero](https://www.xero.com/us/pricing/)** — Cloud accounting software for small businesses with AI-assisted bank reconciliation, invoicing, and expense tracking. `Paid`
- **[Zeni](https://www.zeni.ai/pricing)** — AI-driven bookkeeping platform for startups that automates transaction categorization, bill pay, and financial reporting. `Paid`

## AI Tools for Invoice Processing & Accounts Payable Automation

- **[Rossum](https://rossum.ai/pricing)** — AI document-processing platform that extracts and validates invoice data for finance teams integrating with ERPs like SAP and Oracle. `Paid`
- **[Stampli](https://www.stampli.com)** — AI-powered procure-to-pay platform that automates invoice coding, approvals, and vendor payments for finance and AP teams. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Tipalti](https://tipalti.com)** — Global AP automation platform handling invoice processing, tax compliance, and mass payments for finance teams paying suppliers worldwide. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Vic.ai](https://vic.ai)** — Autonomous AP platform that uses AI to process invoices, match purchase orders, and route approvals for enterprise finance teams. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`

- **[Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/index.html)** — Cloud platform using AI agents to calculate, file, and manage sales tax, VAT, and compliance obligations for businesses. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Bright!Tax](https://brighttax.com/)** — CPA-led service that prepares US federal tax returns and FBAR filings for American expats living abroad. `Paid`

- **[Keeper](https://www.keepertax.com/)** — AI-powered expense-tracking and tax-filing app pairing automated deduction discovery with human accountants for self-employed users. `Paid`
- **[Numeral](https://www.numeral.com/pricing)** — Automates US sales tax, VAT, and GST registration, filing, and remittance for e-commerce and SaaS businesses. `Freemium`

- **[Daloopa](https://daloopa.com/)** — AI platform that auto-extracts and updates sourced financial data from filings and earnings materials for equity analysts building models. `Freemium`
- **[Danelfin](https://danelfin.com/)** — AI stock-scoring platform that rates stocks 1-10 on predicted outperformance and risk, aimed at retail and active individual investors. `Freemium`
- **[Fiscal.ai (formerly Finchat.io)](https://fiscal.ai/)** — AI research terminal with chat-based Q&A over company filings, transcripts, and financials, for equity investors and analysts. `Freemium`
- **[Hebbia](https://www.hebbia.com/)** — Enterprise AI search and analysis tool that lets bankers, analysts, and consultants query large sets of documents and data at once. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Koyfin](https://www.koyfin.com/)** — Financial data and portfolio analytics platform with AI-generated earnings call transcript summaries, for advisors and investors. `Freemium`
- **[PortfolioPilot](https://portfoliopilot.com/)** — AI financial advisor app that analyzes a linked investment portfolio for risk, fees, and tax efficiency, for self-directed investors. `Freemium`

- **[Copilot Money](https://copilot.money/)** — Budgeting and net-worth tracking app for Apple devices that uses AI to auto-categorize transactions and investments. `Paid`
- **[Monarch Money](https://www.monarch.com/)** — Subscription budgeting and net-worth app with an AI assistant for spending Q&A, built for individuals and couples managing money together. `Paid`
- **[Origin](https://useorigin.com/)** — AI financial advisor app that tracks spending, investments, and net worth while offering SEC-regulated automated planning guidance. `Paid`
- **[PocketGuard](https://pocketguard.com/)** — Budgeting app that uses an algorithm to show how much money is safe to spend after bills and goals, for people wanting simplified tracking. `Freemium`
- **[Rocket Money](https://www.rocketmoney.com/)** — Budgeting and subscription-cancellation app with a free tracking tier and a pay-what-you-choose premium plan for bill negotiation. `Freemium`
